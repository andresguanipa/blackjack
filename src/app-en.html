Learn to count cards in blackjack, step by step: shoe and single deck.

1 · The value of each card

+12 3 4 5 6
07 8 9
−110 J Q K A

Low cards help the dealer; high cards help you. When lots of low cards come out, the shoe is left rich in high cards and the count goes up.

2 · Running count

Start at 0 after the shuffle. Add the value of every card you see: yours, the other players’ and the dealer’s. Example: 5, K, 3, 8, A → +1 −1 +1 0 −1 = 0.

Tip: cards that cancel out in pairs (one high, one low) don’t need to be added.

3 · True count

A +6 is worth much more with 1 deck left than with 6 left. That’s why you divide:

true count = running count ÷ decks remaining

Estimate the decks remaining by looking at the discard tray: if a 6-deck shoe has 2 decks in the discard, about 4 are left. Round the result down.

Suggested order

  1. Master basic strategy with no mistakes.
  2. Practice the card values until they’re automatic.
  3. Count down a full deck: it should end at 0. Goal: under 25 seconds.
  4. Practice the true count and the bet.

Card counting is legal, but a casino can ask you to stop playing. Even done well, the edge is small and you can lose for a long time.
